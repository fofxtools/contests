All Match Results

  • All contest matches - final votes and percentages, sortable and filterable by entrant or contest.
  • All contest pictures - all match pics, filterable by entrant or contest.

Oracle PAA

Points Above Average from the Oracle Challenge - how far each predictor’s match score sat above the field average.

  • All Predictions Ever - every scored prediction, with PAA, filterable by contest or user
  • Average PAA standings - best per-match rate
  • Lifetime PAA standings - career total
Oracle - Teams
  • All Team Match Results Ever - every scored team match, with PAA and member picks, filterable by contest or team
  • Average Team PAA standings - best per-match rate
  • Lifetime Team PAA standings - career total

Elo Ratings

Elo ratings for every entrant, built from all official contest matches and kept in separate pools by type (character, game, series, rivalry, year).

  • Tuned Elo ratings - the vote-share Elo ratings tuned to best match real poll outcomes.
  • Base Elo standings - each entrant links to an individual rating-history graph.
  • Head-to-head comparison (tuned) - chart several entrants’ rating histories together and get an estimated matchup between any two. Example.

Site Feature Notes

  • Each poll-update graph has a Smoothing control (all updates / 1 min / 5 min / 15 min) for the noisier matches. You can also set any interval by hand with &seconds=N in the URL (e.g. &seconds=3600 for hourly).
  • For graphs, you can click on the legend to hide an entrant.
  • A copy of the original site is kept as an archive. Login - stats:stats
