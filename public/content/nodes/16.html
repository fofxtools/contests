The following matches were widely believed to cause much inaccuracy in the regular extrapolated standings, due to the very likely influence of Same Fanbase Factor. These SFF-adjusted standings attempt to rectify this by adjusting the losers upwards by a substantial amount. Note that this amount then "trickles down" and raises up any games that they were beneath them in the bracket tree. The adjustment factor for these games was 60%—a number based on no solid data whatsoever. This was simply something I thought was reasonable and simple. Thus, while it is believed these standings should be more accurate than the un-adjusted standings, they are quite "gimmicked". The games receiving a 60% (i.e., scaled upwards by a factor of 1.6) boost, with the game they lost to in parantheses:
  • Mario 64 (Ocarina of Time)
  • Super Metroid (A Link to the Past)
  • Donkey Kong (The Legend of Zelda)
  • Metroid (Super Mario Bros. 3)
There was also a relatively small upwards adjustment of 7.5% made to The Legend of Zelda (for its match against Super Mario Bros. 3).
