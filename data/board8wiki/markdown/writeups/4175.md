We almost got through the contest without Persona 4's YELLOW YELLOW YELLOW YELLOW YELLOW, but it was not to be.

Of all the games to make it to round 3, Persona 4 was clearly the one that didn't belong. It shows you how bad that whole TWEWY/DR/P4/Braid fourpack was, and P4 did nothing in this match to prove itself. 70-30 in round 3 is bad no matter how you slice it, but it was nice to see just to get the Persona Defense Force to finally shut the F up about that dumb series. Those people are lucky Deus Ex happened, or they would have been the worst fanbase of the contest. They could be good games for all I know, but there's no need to remind people every 15 seconds.

As for RE4, the game that actually deserved to make it this far, it made a nice recovery from that embarrassing percentage bleed against Vice City. This match paired with FFX's terrible showing on Golden Sun meant the RE4 \> FFX upset train had a conductor once again.
