## Ulti's Analysis

This match was interesting for about 5 minutes when Lenneth was winning early, but then the second update hit and things returned to normal. We expected Cube \> Claptrap \> Lenneth, and that's what we got---

Er wait a sec, is that Lenneth Freaking Valkyrie going down by 150 and then making a comeback attempt overnight? F\*\*\*in baller! Valkyrie Profile is such an unbelievable gem in an ocean of JRPG junk, and it's nice to see Lenneth making waves in a contest.

She would eventually succumb to Borderlands' day vote, but nothing can take away Lenneth getting some notice for us VP fans. That might be the best of all the unsung JRPGs there is.

Also worth noting here is that even with all the "joke characters" having sophomore slumps, Weighted Companion Cube seems to be just fine and might not ever lose much strength. Portal is just a different animal.
