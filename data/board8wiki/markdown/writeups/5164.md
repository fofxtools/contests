## Ulti's Analysis

This was another of the many "thank god for 12 hour matches early" kind of polls, because this was one of the most predictable matches of the entire contest. You'll get that a lot in any seeding contest, but the craziness of Summer 2013 definitely did not happen early. Luigi crushed, the end.

I will however use this space to defend FF12 a little bit, since Balthier is the s\*\*\*. Yeah, the plot sucks until the Occuria reveal what's up. Yeah, the licenses are stupid. But the gameplay is fun, the music is decent enough, Balthier is Balthier, and FF12 has the best optional content in the entire series. That counts for a lot, especially the hunts. Doing all of those was a BLAST, and by itself is almost convincing me to replay the game again.

Doesn't make for a good contest match, but hey!
