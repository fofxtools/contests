Of all the divisional finals matches, this was the one featuring the oddball seeding. Everything else was 1 vs 2 or 1 vs 3. This poll was also really popular for some reason, because the 63000 votes was very high compared to a lot of other day matches.

We all know the stats from LOL 4-ways are illegitimate and had more than enough proof of this before this match took place, but it's still funny how badly Zelda can dominate 1 on 1. In 2009's second round, Kingdom Hearts 2 was less than 1000 votes away from RE4 for second place and was only 2600 votes away from Twilight Princess for first.

Then you enter these two into a 1 on 1, and Kingdom Hearts 2 gets its face rocked off. To be fair this wasn't all that far away from how last year's stats define it, but Zelda 1 on 1 is really something else and it's almost admirable KH2 was even able to break 40% on it.
