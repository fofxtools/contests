## Ulti's Analysis

Due to this contest having an odd number of matches in rounds 1 and 2 and ending at awkward times, we were given two mid-contest bonus matches. The first of which was a last place affair between the three round 1 characters that did the worst, because if there is anything round 1 needed it was one more match to determine the ultimate last place guy.

We all knew who that was going in, Chester is a champ like that, but there was something hysterical about Caim getting 55% of the vote while only getting 8880 votes. But hey, Chester got a total of 24 hours to make some history, so it can't be all bad. The other great thing here is Luster Soldier's polls on the board decided all of our bonus matches, yet somehow Allen's wife found the time to talk smack on the dude after the contest ended. The whole thing was just great.

And with that, round 2 (and somehow, the contest's entire stretch run) was upon us!
