It's rare you see Zelda ever get embarrassed, especially 1 on 1, but that's what happened here. Wind Waker started this poll off over 70%, and was bled all the way down to below 61%. This simply does not happen to Zelda games, so either Wind Waker hate hasn't fully died down, Assassin's Creed 2 is really legit, or a combination of both.

Notable here is we finally have a decent read on the Assassin's Creed series. Altair's contest matches are all about what picture he has, no one knew who Ezio was in the last character battle (Simon Belmont, a 15 seed, was favored to beat him in brackets) and Assassin's Creed's loss to a weakened MGS4 doesn't really matter. But AC2 bleeding 10% off of a main line Zelda title? That should open some eyes for future contests.
