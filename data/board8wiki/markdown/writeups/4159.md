Never change, Board 8. I have much more than this subject, but I'm saving it for the Prime/Half-Life 2 writeup.

MGS4 started off fairly poorly in this match, and it would only get worse after the slow start. Diablo 2 bled MGS's percentage all throughout the night, and by the end it was clear this wasn't the same Brawl-slayer MGS4 from last year. It was something much weaker, and in the very next match we would see exactly how much trouble this series was in.

But let me give Diablo 2 some hats-off praise here. Western RPGs have trended way up on GameFAQs for quite awhile, but Diablo 2 remains the best in its genre. That game was the perfect storm of gameplay, community, and a soma-based reward system without having to sacrifice your entire life to get things. When in doubt, buy it. Sure the economy was eventually based off duped Stones of Jordan and Dupewords, but it all added to the fun. Even now I still post on d2jsp (the best forum on the internet) just to see what's been doing on (they're all waiting on Diablo 3).
