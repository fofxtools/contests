Before the contest, this match was relatively hyped for upset potential. Sonic only scored 55% on Kirby in 2010, and most assumed Robotnik would hold Sonic down while Meta Knight would boost Kirby up some more.

"Whoops"

Not only did none of this happen in yet another match that was over in seconds, but Sonic went up by 7% from the year before with a character most people hate by his side. This goes to show you that iconic rivalries really did matter in this contest, while the vast majority of Kirby's strength comes not from his games, but from Smash Bros games.
