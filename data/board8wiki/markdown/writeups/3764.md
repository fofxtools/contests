## Ulti's Analysis

This was a really strange match. Roxas was a lock to win it, but for the second time 4chan decided to have a huge rally for a Team Fortress 2 character. It didn't help Heavy get anywhere close to winning, but he managed to win a few updates overnight and pull Roxas's percentage down to 55% from 61%. Even with how bad Kingdom Hearts characters do overnight, that's a pretty large dropoff. Then the morning vote hit, and Roxas flew back upwards until the end.

Trends aside, the only exciting thing about this match was the picture. When Roxas of all people can look as hard as Heavy, you know there's a good picsmith responsible.
