Why hello there King of the ASV, haven't seen you in awhile. How have you been?

This match was like reuniting with a long-lost friend, and I feel like it was only possible due to 1 on 1 matches being back. Say whatever you will about Halo, but its ASV is really something else.

Just to give you an idea of how ridiculous this truly was, this was the score of the match 1 hour in:

Uncharted 2: Among Thieves - 3115 \[50.04%\]

Halo: Combat Evolved - 3110 \[49.96%\]

Total Votes - 6225

That turned into a near 54-46 match over the next 11 hours, thanks to Halo staying at or just below 60% intake for a good deal of updates. Even with this Uncharted 2 had a pretty good contest, but you can't help feeling sorry when someone or something is on the receiving end of one of Halo's steroid ASVs. A lot of people breathed a sigh of relief the Fallout 3/Halo match was a night match, because no one wants any part of that ASV.
