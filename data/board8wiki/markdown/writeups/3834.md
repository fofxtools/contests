Samus ended up with a doubling here, which is very good. Remember, Auron failed to even break 60% on Sub-Zero a few years ago.

But then she fell from that insane pace overnight and ended with 67%, which put her from "above Cloud" to "longshot to pull off an upset on him". Even still, this was an absolutely amazing match for her and throws more proof onto the "Samus is probably stronger than Mario indirectly" pile.
