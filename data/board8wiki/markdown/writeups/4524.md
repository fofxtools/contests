I love how I preach about 80% being hard and this is like the 213th match in a row where we saw someone flirt with that line or break it.

Spoilers for those who haven't listened to The Show yet (not up as of this writing): I named Dan and Sagat my turd of the contest. I honestly expected something out of these two, or at least Sagat, and instead we get a performance that makes people think Chris and Wesker had a shot at beating the bosses. It was one of those quasi-joke rallies that actually worked, and Board 8 nominated it enough to have the pair avoid the vote-ins entirely. I'm a Street Fighter fanboy and LOVE these two characters, I make no secret of this, so yeah I was really disappointed.

Chris and Wesker are no slouches, don't get me wrong, but those two getting 80% on someone means something royally effed happened.
