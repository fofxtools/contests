Lots of stuff to talk about here, and I'll start with the actual match for once. A Pyrrhic Victory is a battle that one wins in war, but at such great losses that it spells eventual doom for the army and the nation. The best example of a Pyrrhic Victory is the Battle of Thermopylae, and I believe the term even comes from that particular exchange.

Maybe you've seen [the movie that got made about it?](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0416449/)

(Holy s\*\*\*, that movie is five years old already? I am getting old really fast, I remember that launch like it was yesterday.)

To the point. Yeah Samus won this match, but only 61% against Terra and Kefka, really? People guessed all pre-contest that Ridley would completely screw her eventually, and they were right. There was zero chance Samus was going to make the semifinals once this match happened, and it's worth noting that it took FF6's piss-awful day vote to prevent Samus from falling below 60%. A lot predicted that Ryu and Ken would be able to take advantage of Ridley being an anchor, but it would eventually be the trainers. Right division, wrong rivalry.
