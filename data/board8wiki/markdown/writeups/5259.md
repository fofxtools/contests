## Ulti's Analysis

Of all the divisional finals, this was by far the simplest and had nothing unexpected happen at all. L-Block tried making a push early, but wasn't even able to hold second place over Tifa for long before getting completely swamped. It gave the board 24 hours to breathe and relax, and this was the one match among that stretch of 8 I talked about that didn't have a Pokemon in it. So if you're one of those people that hates Pokemon, this was the match for you.

Overall, it was clear in minutes that Samus was taking care of business, that L-Block would not recapture the past glory, and that Tifa was not going to give us a repeat of her near-upset of Samus from the female half of the 2006 contest.

Just a simple beating, nothing more and nothing less. We can't have EVERY match be nuts, right? Moving on.
