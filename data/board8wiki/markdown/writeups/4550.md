It led to a massive argument about whether or not Link should be removed, and for whatever reason people keep playing the "popularity contest and Link is popular, u mad" card and comparing him to the Yankees.

To that, some people are really effin dumb. The Yankees actually have a chance to lose in the playoffs, as they prove in the first round almost every year these days. Link will never lose a normal match ever again barring a complete miracle (which happened with L-Block already), otherwise he's a lock to win every contest. Link being retired is about 3 years overdue, though thankfully it finally looks like we're getting him out of here with how this contest turned out. Ammy would hardly be the only character to get godstomped to a "just f\*\*\*ing retire Link already, this is stupid" degree.
