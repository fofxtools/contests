The only exciting part of this match was the match picture. Go to the link and hit refresh a few times to see what I mean.

And yes, I'm aware that Chrono Trigger put up a fight for a good hour of the poll. But Sephiroth also put up a good fight against Cloud before finally getting rolled over. The board has a way of voting against the character or game they know will win, but in the end, their votes don't matter as the true favorite's strength shines through. Still, putting up a good fight against FF7 is no easy task, so props to Chrono Trigger for at least refusing to just roll over and die.

Overall, I felt that this contest was quite fun. But I also feel that CJayC really screwed the thing up with the poll starting time being at 3 AM EST and the layout changes. This contest could have easily added to the progression that we were growing with the first two contests, but instead we took a step backwards. Hopefully things are done to help rectify this with the upcoming Summer Contest, and from the initial looks of the bracket, it looks to be that way. Good luck everyone, and thanks again to everyone that helps make this contest what it is =)
