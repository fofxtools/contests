## Ulti's Analysis

There was some buzz Seph could lose this match coming from people who overestimated his downfall a bit. Basically, it went "hey Seph always loses the board vote, and if Falcon is leading early people might rally". Stuff like that. It actually wasn't a bad theory and Falcon did lead for a few seconds, but Sephiroth proceeded to stomp the crap out of him shortly after. This was never a match after the first few seconds, and we have a fantastic comparison point for this one.

<https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/poll/3836-midgar-division-round-3-cloud-strife-vs-captain-falcon>
