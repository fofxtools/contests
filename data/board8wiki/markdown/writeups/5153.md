## Ulti's Analysis

There was a minor debate about Lucina winning this match until Tharja's awful performance in her earlier match, but for the most part Dracula winning this was a lock. There was too much going for him, namely his showing in the Rivalry Rumble and this being a night match, and Lucina didn't have much going for her.

This match was mainly notable for Caim's historically awful performance, even among a field of 243 loaded with historically awful performances. For a time, Caim was threatening being the worst character in contest history. But he, uh...." recovered" his percentage later in the match to get up to a "respectable" level.

He still set all sorts of horrendous records though, because it takes a special kind of suck to only score 1586 votes in a contest match. And even with all of that, Caim wasn't even bottom 5 in the raw LOL, X-stats. This contest had some horrible characters in it.
