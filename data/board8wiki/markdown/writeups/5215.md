## Ulti's Analysis

This was supposed to be our last 1 seed beatdown of round 1, but it became the lone entertaining 1 seed match of the contest.

The reason? This poll: <http://www.gamefaqs.com/poll/5141>

Magikarp had a pre-planned rally in this contest, and under different circumstances he might have pulled the crazy out-of-nowhere upset. But Mega Man had the big Smash U announcement pre-contest, so Magikarp only got 5 minutes of being tied for the lead. After that, Mega Man completely stomped this match the way any 1 seed should stomp any match.

Well, "stomp" being relative. Magikarp still ended up with 37% on him, but poor Geno just got absolutely swamped. This may have been the first time Mega Man overcame a Pokemon threat, but it would absolutely not be the last.
