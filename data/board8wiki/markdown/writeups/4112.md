We learned very little here. Persona 4 needed to totally wreck house here to make it the favorite next round, and even then people might have just blamed Braid for being fodder or something. What we can look at however is total votes. 40k after the morning match was super close could have potentially meant trouble for Persona 4, but it was one of those things we wouldn't know until match time.

Both Personas winning matches in this contest was awesome for the game's fans (all 25 of them), but it's hard for me to look at this match, go "It couldn't even double a download-only game?" and think Persona 4 disappointed. It's not like Braid was Mega Man 9 and had franchise power to draw from.
