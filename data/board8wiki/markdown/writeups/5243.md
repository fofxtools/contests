## Ulti's Analysis

Samus and Catherine in a poll together is cute if you're into anime fanfiction garbage, but as a contest match in round 2? I doubt that would actually happen in a real 1 on 1 setting, but let an old contest vet have his delusions of a 1 on 1 contest happening again down the line somewhere =/
