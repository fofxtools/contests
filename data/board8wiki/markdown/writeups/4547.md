As for the match, this was never going to be in doubt but given the Trainers going into total godmode on Wa/Luigi it was up to the fighters to prove themselves worthy of taking down the trainers.

It's easy to call confirmation bias here and say 78% on Luigi just demolishes what the fighters did here, but I don't think "demolish" is really the right word. The trainers were favorites after these two matches and they were pretty clearly going to win, but not by some absurd amount. Ryu and Ken got every bit the rivalry boost this contest that the trainers did.
